== Description ==

Built for premium news, media, and content brands, [Pushly](https://pushly.com) gives editorial and audience teams a direct way to bring readers back to your site without relying entirely on search or social platforms.

With Pushly, you can send timely browser notifications when new content is published, promote high-value stories, recirculate readers, and create targeted messaging experiences that keep audiences engaged on-site and beyond the first visit.

Pushly is designed for publishers that need more than a basic notification plugin. Our platform supports high-volume publishing workflows, flexible messaging strategies, and hands-on support from a team that understands digital media.

= Why Pushly =

* Reach readers directly with web push notifications
* Re-engage visitors with targeted in-app messaging
* Drive traffic back to your site with timely content alerts
* Support editorial, audience, and growth strategies from one platform
* Reduce dependence on changing social and search algorithms
* Launch quickly with an easy WordPress integration and responsive support

= Features =

* **Web Push Notifications** — Send browser-based notifications to bring readers back to your site when new content is published or important stories break.
* **In-App Messaging** — Engage visitors while they are on your site with targeted messages that support recirculation, conversion, and retention.
* **Editorial Alerts** — Help editors and audience teams distribute breaking news, top stories, and featured content quickly.
* **Audience Re-Engagement** — Keep your brand in front of readers after they leave your site and encourage repeat visits.
* **Publisher-Focused Workflow** — Built for the needs of news, media, and content organizations.
* **Hands-On Support** — Get help from a real team with experience supporting premium publishers.

You create the content. Pushly helps make sure your audience sees it.

== Installation ==

For detailed setup instructions including screenshots, see the [WordPress integration guide](https://documentation.pushly.com/integration/web-push/wordpress-plugin).

1. Install Pushly from the WordPress.org plugin directory
2. Activate the plugin
3. Click the "Pushly" menu item in the sidebar
4. Enter your SDK Key and configure notification settings
5. Save the form
